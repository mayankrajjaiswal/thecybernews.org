## What is it?
Often called "Pig Butchering" in the cybersecurity community, this is a highly sophisticated, long-term scam. The scammer (often operating out of international crime syndicates) spends weeks or months building a friendly or romantic relationship with you over text messages. Once they have gained your absolute trust, they casually mention how much money they are making trading cryptocurrency and offer to teach you. They convince you to deposit money into a fake trading platform that they control, eventually draining your entire life savings.

## How it works (The Teardown)
1. **The "Wrong Number" Text:** You receive a casual text message from an unknown number: *"Hi Sarah, are we still on for lunch tomorrow?"* When you reply that they have the wrong number, they apologize profusely, strike up a polite conversation, and ask to be friends.
2. **The Grooming (Fattening the Pig):** Over the next month, they text you every day. They send pictures of their luxurious lifestyle, their pets, and their meals. They seem like a genuinely wealthy, successful, and kind person.
3. **The Hook:** They mention a "guaranteed" trading node or "insider tip" for Bitcoin or Ethereum. They offer to help you set up an account so you can make money too.
4. **The Illusion:** They direct you to a website that looks exactly like a legitimate trading platform (like Coinbase or Binance). You deposit $500. The fake dashboard shows your money growing to $1,500 in three days. They even let you withdraw $100 to prove it's "real."
5. **The Slaughter:** Believing the system works, you transfer $50,000 of your retirement savings. The dashboard shows you are a millionaire! But when you try to withdraw the funds, the platform says you must pay a 20% "Tax Fee" first. Once you realize it's a scam, the "friend" blocks your number, the website disappears, and your money is gone forever.

## How to identify this scam
* **Unsolicited Wealth Advice:** If a stranger on the internet, a dating app, or a "wrong number" text message starts talking about crypto investments, it is a scam 100% of the time.
* **Pressure to move to WhatsApp/Telegram:** They always try to move the conversation away from the original dating app or SMS to encrypted apps where they cannot be easily traced or banned.

## How to avoid it
* Never take investment advice from someone you have only ever met online.
* If you want to buy cryptocurrency, only use major, publicly traded, heavily regulated exchanges (like Coinbase or Kraken). Never download a "special" trading app sent to you via a text message link.

## What to do if you are affected
1. **Stop all contact:** Do not tell the scammer you know they are fake. Simply block them. Do not pay any "withdrawal fees" or "taxes" to get your money back; you will only lose more money.
2. **Report to Authorities:** Crypto is incredibly difficult to recover, but the FBI (via IC3.gov) tracks these syndicates. Provide them with all the wallet addresses and websites the scammer used.
3. **Beware of Recovery Scams:** You will likely be targeted by "Recovery Hackers" on Instagram or Reddit who claim they can hack the scammer and get your money back for a fee. **These are the exact same scammers trying to steal from you a second time.**
