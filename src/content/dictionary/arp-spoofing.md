## What is ARP Spoofing?

A type of attack in which a malicious actor sends falsified ARP (Address Resolution Protocol) messages over a local area network.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.
