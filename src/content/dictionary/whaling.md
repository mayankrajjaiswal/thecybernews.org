## What is Whaling?

A specific kind of phishing attack that is targeted at high-profile executives like the CEO or CFO of a company.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like a scammer trying to reel in the biggest, most valuable 'fish' in the company rather than the small ones.
