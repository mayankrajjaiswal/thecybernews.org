## What is RADIUS?

A networking protocol that provides centralized Authentication, Authorization, and Accounting management for users who connect and use a network service.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like a central security office in a large office building that checks your badge before letting you access any specific floor or room.
