## What is SSL / TLS?

Secure Sockets Layer and Transport Layer Security. Cryptographic protocols designed to provide communications security over a computer network.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.
