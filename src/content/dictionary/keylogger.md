## The Short Version
**Keylogger** is a type of surveillance software that has the capability to record every keystroke you make to a log file.

## The Everyday Analogy
Think of it like an invisible spy standing over your shoulder writing down everything you type.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept is crucial for protecting your digital identity and recognizing modern cyber threats before they cause damage.
