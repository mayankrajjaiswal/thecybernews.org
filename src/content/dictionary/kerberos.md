## What is Kerberos?

A computer network authentication protocol that works on the basis of tickets to allow nodes communicating over a non-secure network to prove their identity to one another.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like a coat check ticket or movie theater ticket: you show your ID once, get a ticket, and then use that ticket to get your coat back without showing ID again.
