## What is IaaS?

Infrastructure as a Service. A form of cloud computing that provides virtualized computing resources over the internet.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like renting an empty plot of land where you have to build your own house, but you do not own the land itself.
