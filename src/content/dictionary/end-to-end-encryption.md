## The Short Version
**End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)** is a system of communication where only the communicating users can read the messages.

## The Everyday Analogy
Think of it like sending a locked box in the mail where only the recipient has the key to open it.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept is crucial for protecting your digital identity and recognizing modern cyber threats before they cause damage.
