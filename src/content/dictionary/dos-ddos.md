## What is DoS vs DDoS?

Denial of Service (DoS) and Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS). An attack meant to shut down a machine or network, making it inaccessible to its intended users.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like a massive crowd of people rushing the entrance of a store all at once, preventing actual paying customers from getting inside.
