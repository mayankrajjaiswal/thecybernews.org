## What is Syslogs?

A standard for message logging. It allows separation of the software that generates messages, the system that stores them, and the software that reports and analyzes them.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like an airplane's black box or a ship's captain's log, recording every significant event that happens.
