## What is LAN vs WAN?

A LAN connects computers in a small area (like a home). A WAN connects networks across a large area (like the Internet).

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like the intercom system inside a single building (LAN) vs the global telephone network (WAN).
