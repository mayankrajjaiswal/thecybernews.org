## What is VLAN (Virtual LAN)?

Any broadcast domain that is partitioned and isolated in a computer network at the data link layer.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like putting invisible soundproof walls in an open-plan office so different departments can only hear their own team.
