## What is CIA Triad?

Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability. A model designed to guide policies for information security within an organization.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like a three-legged stool; if any leg (keeping secrets, preventing tampering, staying accessible) breaks, the whole security posture falls over.
