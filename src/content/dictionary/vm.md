## What is Virtual Machine (VM)?

An emulation of a computer system. Virtual machines are based on computer architectures and provide functionality of a physical computer.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like running a computer inside another computer; similar to having a picture-in-picture on your TV.
