## What is FTP and SFTP?

Protocols used for the transfer of computer files between a client and server on a computer network. SFTP adds a layer of security.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like a standard moving company (FTP) vs an armored moving company (SFTP) transporting your belongings.
