## What is Rogue Access Point?

A wireless access point that has been installed on a secure network without explicit authorization from a local network administrator.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like an employee secretly plugging in their own Wi-Fi router in the office so they can use their iPad, unknowingly leaving a backdoor open for hackers.
