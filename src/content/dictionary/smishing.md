## The Short Version
**Smishing** is a form of phishing that uses mobile phone text messages to lure victims into revealing personal information or downloading malware.

## The Everyday Analogy
Think of it like a scammer sending a fake letter to your physical mailbox instead of your email.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept is crucial for protecting your digital identity and recognizing modern cyber threats before they cause damage.
