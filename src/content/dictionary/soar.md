## What is SOAR?

Security Orchestration, Automation, and Response. A solution stack of compatible software programs that allow an organization to collect data about security threats and respond to them automatically.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like a smart home security system that doesn't just ring an alarm, but automatically locks the doors and calls the police when a break-in is detected.
