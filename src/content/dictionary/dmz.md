## What is DMZ (Demilitarized Zone)?

A physical or logical subnetwork that contains and exposes an organization's external-facing services to an untrusted network, usually a larger network such as the Internet.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like the lobby of a bank: the public can enter the lobby to use ATMs, but the vault and employee offices remain behind locked doors.
