## What is Man in the Middle (MITM)?

An attack where the attacker secretly relays and possibly alters the communications between two parties who believe they are directly communicating with each other.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like a postal worker secretly opening your letters, reading them, maybe changing the words, sealing them back up, and delivering them.
