## What is CSRF?

Cross-Site Request Forgery. An attack that forces an end user to execute unwanted actions on a web application in which they're currently authenticated.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like someone forging your signature on a check while you are already logged into your banking app.
