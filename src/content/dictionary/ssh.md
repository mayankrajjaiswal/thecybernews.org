## What is SSH (Secure Shell)?

A cryptographic network protocol for operating network services securely over an unsecured network.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like a secure, soundproof tunnel between two offices that allows workers to pass information without anyone outside overhearing.
