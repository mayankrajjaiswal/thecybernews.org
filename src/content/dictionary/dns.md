## What is DNS?

The phonebook of the Internet. It translates domain names to IP addresses so browsers can load Internet resources.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like looking up a friend's name in your phone's contacts to find their phone number.
