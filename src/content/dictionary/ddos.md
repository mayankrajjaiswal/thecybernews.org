## The Short Version
**DDoS Attack** is a malicious attempt to disrupt the normal traffic of a targeted server by overwhelming the target with a flood of internet traffic.

## The Everyday Analogy
Think of it like a thousand fake customers crowding into a small store so real customers cannot get in.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept is crucial for protecting your digital identity and recognizing modern cyber threats before they cause damage.
