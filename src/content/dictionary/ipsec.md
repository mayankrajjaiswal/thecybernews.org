## What is IPsec?

Internet Protocol Security. A secure network protocol suite that authenticates and encrypts the packets of data sent over an internet protocol network.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like putting a secure lock on every single piece of mail sent between two office buildings (often used in VPNs).
