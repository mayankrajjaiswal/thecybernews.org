## The Short Version
**Single Sign-On (SSO)** is an authentication scheme that allows a user to log in with a single id and password to any of several related, yet independent, software systems.

## The Everyday Analogy
Think of it like a master key that opens every door in an office building.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept is crucial for protecting your digital identity and recognizing modern cyber threats before they cause damage.
