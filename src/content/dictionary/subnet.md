## What is Subnet?

A logical subdivision of an IP network. The practice of dividing a network into two or more networks is called subnetting.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like dividing a large city into smaller neighborhoods, each with its own zip code, to make mail delivery faster and more organized.
