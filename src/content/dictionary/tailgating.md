## What is Tailgating?

A physical security breach in which an unauthorized person follows an authorized person into a secured area.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like holding the door open out of politeness for someone walking behind you into an office building, not realizing they don't work there.
