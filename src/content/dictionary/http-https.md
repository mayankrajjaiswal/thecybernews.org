## What is HTTP and HTTPS?

The foundation of data communication for the World Wide Web. HTTPS is the secure version.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like sending a postcard (HTTP) vs sending a sealed, tamper-proof envelope (HTTPS).
