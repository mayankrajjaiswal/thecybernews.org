## The Short Version
**Password Manager** is a software application designed to store and manage online credentials.

## The Everyday Analogy
Think of it like an unbreakable digital vault that holds all your keys.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept is crucial for protecting your digital identity and recognizing modern cyber threats before they cause damage.
