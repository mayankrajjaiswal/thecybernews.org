## What is Group Policy?

A feature that controls the working environment of user accounts and computer accounts in an organization.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like the house rules set by parents (e.g., no TV after 9 PM) that apply to all kids in the house.
