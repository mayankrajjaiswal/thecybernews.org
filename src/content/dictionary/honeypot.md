## What is Honeypot?

A computer security mechanism set to detect, deflect, or, in some manner, counteract attempts at unauthorized use of information systems.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like a decoy safe filled with fake money left in plain sight to distract robbers from the real safe.
