## The Short Version
**Passkey** is a digital credential tied to a user account and a specific website or application, replacing the need for passwords.

## The Everyday Analogy
Think of it like using your actual fingerprint instead of a written signature to prove who you are.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept is crucial for protecting your digital identity and recognizing modern cyber threats before they cause damage.
