## What is RDP (Remote Desktop Protocol)?

A proprietary protocol developed by Microsoft which provides a user with a graphical interface to connect to another computer over a network connection.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like flying a drone with a camera and remote control; you are physically here, but controlling and seeing exactly what the drone sees over there.
