## The Short Version
**Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** is a security system that requires more than one method of authentication to verify a user.

## The Everyday Analogy
Think of it like needing both a key and a thumbprint to open a safe.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept is crucial for protecting your digital identity and recognizing modern cyber threats before they cause damage.
