## The Short Version
**Zero Trust** is a security framework requiring all users, whether in or outside the organization's network, to be authenticated, authorized, and continuously validated.

## The Everyday Analogy
Think of it like a high-security facility that checks your badge at every single hallway, not just the front door.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept is crucial for protecting your digital identity and recognizing modern cyber threats before they cause damage.
