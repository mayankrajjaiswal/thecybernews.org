## What is Principle of Least Privilege?

The practice of limiting access rights for users to the bare minimum permissions they need to perform their work.

## Why it matters
Understanding this concept helps in grasping how modern networks and security systems operate, providing a foundation for more advanced topics.

## Real-world Analogy
> Like giving an employee the key only to the supply closet they need, rather than a master key to the whole building.
