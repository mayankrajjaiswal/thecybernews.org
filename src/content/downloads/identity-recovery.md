## 🚨 IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (Do these first)

### 1. Freeze Your Credit Reports
Stop criminals from opening new credit cards or loans in your name. You must contact all three major credit bureaus individually:
*   [ ] **Equifax:** 1-800-685-1111 (or Equifax.com)
*   [ ] **Experian:** 1-888-397-3742 (or Experian.com)
*   [ ] **TransUnion:** 1-888-909-8872 (or TransUnion.com)

### 2. Contact Your Primary Bank
*   [ ] Call the fraud number on the back of your debit/credit cards.
*   [ ] Ask them to freeze your current cards and issue new ones with different numbers.
*   [ ] Ask to place a "Fraud Alert" on your checking account.

### 3. Secure Your Email
If hackers have your email, they can reset passwords for all your other accounts.
*   [ ] Log into your primary email (Gmail, Outlook).
*   [ ] Change the password immediately.
*   [ ] Turn on Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

---

## 📄 SECONDARY ACTIONS (Within 48 hours)

### 4. File an Identity Theft Report
*   [ ] Go to **IdentityTheft.gov** (The official FTC website).
*   [ ] Fill out the report. This creates an official "Identity Theft Report" which you will need to prove to debt collectors that you didn't incur the fraudulent charges.

### 5. Review Your Credit Reports
*   [ ] Go to **AnnualCreditReport.com** (The only federally authorized free credit report site).
*   [ ] Download your reports from all three bureaus.
*   [ ] Highlight any accounts or addresses you do not recognize.

### 6. File a Police Report
*   [ ] Go to your local police station with a copy of your FTC Identity Theft Report, a government ID, and proof of your address.
*   [ ] Ask to file a report. Get a copy of the police report; banks may require this to clear fraudulent charges.

---

> **A Note on "Credit Monitoring" Services:**
> You do not need to pay $20/month for identity theft protection like LifeLock. Freezing your credit at the three bureaus is 100% free by federal law and is much more effective than monitoring, because a freeze *prevents* the account from being opened in the first place.
