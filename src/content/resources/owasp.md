## Overview
OWASP is the global standard for application security. It is a massive, global community of developers and security researchers who collaborate to figure out exactly how hackers are breaking into websites, and how to stop them.

## Why It Matters
If you are learning how to code, or if your company builds software, OWASP is your Bible. They are most famous for the **OWASP Top 10**, a regularly updated report outlining security concerns for web application security, focusing on the 10 most critical risks (like Injection attacks and Broken Authentication).

## Popular Resources
* **The OWASP Top 10:** The ultimate checklist for securing a website.
* **OWASP ZAP (Zed Attack Proxy):** A free, open-source penetration testing tool used by security professionals around the world to find vulnerabilities in web applications.
