## The Pizza as a Service Analogy

Understanding cloud models is easiest when compared to eating pizza.

### 1. On-Premises (Made at Home)
You buy the ingredients, the oven, the gas, the dining table, and you do the cooking and the cleaning. You manage everything.

### 2. IaaS: Infrastructure as a Service (Take and Bake)
The grocery store provides the pre-made pizza. You still have to use your own oven, gas, and table to cook and eat it. 
*Example: Renting a raw virtual machine on AWS.*

### 3. PaaS: Platform as a Service (Pizza Delivery)
The pizza place makes it and cooks it for you. You just provide the dining table and the drinks.
*Example: A platform where developers just upload their code, and the cloud handles the servers.*

### 4. SaaS: Software as a Service (Dine-in Restaurant)
You show up at the restaurant. They provide the pizza, the oven, the table, the drinks, and do the cleaning. You just consume the service.
*Example: Gmail or Netflix.*
