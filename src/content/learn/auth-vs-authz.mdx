## The Difference is Crucial

These two terms sound similar but represent two completely different steps in security (often referred to together as IAM: Identity and Access Management).

### Authentication (Who are you?)
Authentication is the process of verifying your identity. 
- *Analogy:* Showing your passport at border control.
- *Examples:* Entering a password, scanning your fingerprint, or using an MFA app on your phone.

### Authorization (What are you allowed to do?)
Once the system knows who you are, it must decide what you are allowed to access.
- *Analogy:* After passing border control, your visa dictates whether you can work, study, or just travel as a tourist.
- *Examples:* A standard employee can only view their own paystub, while the HR manager is authorized to view everyone's paystubs.

A secure system requires both strong authentication (like MFA) and strict authorization (like the Principle of Least Privilege).
