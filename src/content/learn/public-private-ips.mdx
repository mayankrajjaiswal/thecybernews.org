## What is an IP Address?

An IP (Internet Protocol) address is like a digital home address. Every device connected to the internet needs one so other devices know where to send information.

## Public vs. Private

- **Public IP:** This is the address given to your home router by your Internet Service Provider. It is visible to the entire internet.
- **Private IP:** These are the addresses your router assigns to your personal devices (like your phone, laptop, or smart TV). These are *not* visible to the internet. 

Your router uses a technology called NAT (Network Address Translation) to act as a middleman. When your phone wants to load a website, the router requests it using the Public IP, and then privately hands the website data back to your phone.

## Default Gateway

Your **Default Gateway** is usually your router's private IP address. When a device doesn't know how to reach a destination on the local network, it sends the data to the default gateway, which figures out how to route it to the internet.
